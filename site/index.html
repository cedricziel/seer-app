Flowmark Media

A native Jellyfin and Jellyseerr client for iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV.

What it does

  • Stream movies, shows, and music from any Jellyfin server.
  • Continue watching across devices with up-to-the-second progress sync.
  • Picture-in-Picture playback on iPhone and iPad.
  • Background downloads for offline viewing — even on Apple TV.
  • Multi-server support with iCloud sync.
  • Browse and request new titles via Jellyseerr.
  • Full VoiceOver and Reduce Motion support.

Privacy

The app talks only to the servers you configure. Diagnostic data is fully opt-in. No advertising, no tracking, no third-party analytics. Read the full privacy policy →
