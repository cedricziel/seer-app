Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 May 2026 · App: Flowmark Media (com.cedricziel.seer) · Developer: Cedric Ziel

Summary

Flowmark Media is a client app for Jellyfin and Jellyseerr media servers. The app talks only to the servers you explicitly configure. By default, no data is sent anywhere else. Optional diagnostic data is opt-in and never linked to your identity.

What we collect

By default: nothing

The app does not collect personal information, browsing history, viewing habits, location, contacts, or any usage data unless you explicitly opt in.

Opt-in diagnostics

If you enable Diagnostics in Settings, the app sends anonymous diagnostic data:

  • Crash reports: stack traces, exception types, termination reasons.
  • Performance metrics: app launch time, hang duration, peak memory usage, cumulative CPU usage.
  • Device context: device model identifier (e.g., iPhone15,2), iOS or tvOS version, app version and build number, locale, timezone.

This data is not linked to your identity, your Apple ID, or your Jellyfin account. It is used solely to identify and fix bugs and performance regressions. You can disable it at any time in Settings › Diagnostics. Because reports are anonymous, already-sent data cannot be retroactively deleted — there is no link back to you.

Server credentials

When you connect to a Jellyfin or Jellyseerr server, your username, password or API token, and the server URL are stored in the iOS Keychain. If you have iCloud Keychain enabled, these credentials sync across your Apple devices using Apple's standard end-to-end-encrypted iCloud Keychain mechanism. Credentials are never sent to the app's developer.

Server communication

All communication with your Jellyfin and Jellyseerr servers happens directly between your device and those servers, using the URLs you provide. The app does not proxy, intercept, or relay this traffic through any service operated by the developer.

What we don't do

  • No advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.).
  • No behavioral tracking across apps or services.
  • No data sharing with brokers or partners.
  • No sale of any data.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the change will be announced in the app's release notes and reflected in this document. The Effective date above will update accordingly.

Contact
