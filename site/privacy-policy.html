Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 October 2026 · App: Flowmark Media (com.cedricziel.seer) · Developer: Cedric Ziel

Summary

Flowmark Media is a client app for Jellyfin and Jellyseerr media servers. The app connects directly to the media servers you configure and sends diagnostics to our diagnostics service. Diagnostics are enabled by default to maintain app stability and performance. You can disable them in Settings.

What we collect

Media and account data

We do not use diagnostics to collect your media browsing history, viewing habits, server credentials, location, or contacts. Your media requests go directly to the servers you configure.

Diagnostics and legitimate interest

We process diagnostics on the basis of our legitimate interest under GDPR Article 6(1)(f) in identifying crashes, fixing defects, and maintaining app reliability. Diagnostics are enabled by default, with separate controls for crash reports and performance data in Settings. Existing disabled preferences remain disabled. Reports can include:

  • Crash reports: stack traces, exception types, termination reasons.
  • Performance metrics: app launch time, hang duration, peak memory usage, cumulative CPU usage.
  • Device context: device model identifier (e.g., iPhone15,2), operating system version, app version and build number, and OpenTelemetry SDK version.
  • Diagnostic events: onboarding steps, discovery path, and authentication method, without server addresses or account names.
  • Installation identifier: a random UUID kept in local Keychain storage, without iCloud sync. It links reports from this installation across launches and can survive app reinstallation. It is not an advertising identifier.

Reports are pseudonymous, not completely anonymous. We do not attach your Apple ID or media-server account. Crash messages and stack traces can contain contextual information, so access is restricted to diagnosing app faults. We do not use diagnostics for advertising, cross-service tracking, or profiling.

Diagnostic reports are retained for 30 days. The local installation identifier remains in Keychain until removed; turning off diagnostics stops its use for new reports but does not delete the identifier or already-uploaded reports.

You can object to this processing at any time by disabling either diagnostics category in Server Management › Privacy & Diagnostics. Turning both off stops new diagnostic exports; requests already in flight may finish. This does not affect app functionality. To request access, deletion, or restriction of reports already received, contact us below and include the Diagnostics ID shown in Privacy settings if available. You may lodge a complaint with your data-protection supervisory authority.

Server credentials

When you connect to a Jellyfin or Jellyseerr server, your username, password or API token, and the server URL are stored in the iOS Keychain. If you have iCloud Keychain enabled, these credentials sync across your Apple devices using Apple's standard end-to-end-encrypted iCloud Keychain mechanism. Credentials are never sent to the app's developer.

Server communication

All communication with your Jellyfin and Jellyseerr servers happens directly between your device and those servers, using the URLs you provide. The app does not proxy, intercept, or relay this traffic through any service operated by the developer.

What we don't do

  • No advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.).
  • No behavioral tracking across apps or services.
  • No data sharing with brokers or partners.
  • No sale of any data.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the change will be announced in the app's release notes and reflected in this document. The Effective date above will update accordingly.
