Flowmark Media is a native client for Jellyfin and Jellyseerr, built for iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV.

Browse your library, search across servers, watch in Picture-in-Picture, and download episodes for the flight. Pair it with Jellyseerr to discover what's trending and request new movies and shows without leaving the app.

Designed for people who self-host their media and want a fast, focused, native streaming experience on Apple devices.

FEATURES

• Stream movies, shows, and music from any Jellyfin server
• Continue watching across devices with up-to-the-second progress sync
• Search your full library with instant results
• Picture-in-Picture playback on iPhone and iPad
• Background downloads for offline viewing — even on Apple TV
• Multi-server support: switch between home, family, and friends' servers in one tap
• iCloud sync keeps your servers and credentials in step across devices
• Browse trending and discover new titles via Jellyseerr
• Request movies and shows directly from the discover feed
• Full VoiceOver support and Reduce Motion compliance

PRIVACY

Flowmark Media talks only to the Jellyfin and Jellyseerr servers you configure. Diagnostic data (crashes, performance) is fully opt-in and never linked to your identity. No tracking, no ads, no third-party analytics.

REQUIREMENTS

• A Jellyfin server (10.8+) you have access to
• Optionally, a Jellyseerr instance for media requests
• iOS 26 or tvOS 26 or later
