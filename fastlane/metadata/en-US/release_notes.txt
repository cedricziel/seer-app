Welcome to Flowmark Media — the native Jellyfin and Jellyseerr client for iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV.

This first release includes:
• Stream and continue watching across your Jellyfin library
• Search, library browse, and per-genre discovery
• Picture-in-Picture playback on iPhone and iPad
• Background downloads for offline viewing
• Multi-server support with iCloud sync
• Browse and request new titles via Jellyseerr
• VoiceOver and Reduce Motion support

Thanks for trying it out. Source and feedback at github.com/cedricziel/seer-app.
